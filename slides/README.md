# Five-slide research overview

Use `shared-intelligence-slides.pdf` for reading, `shared-intelligence-slides.pptx` for editing, and `slide-1.png` through `slide-5.png` for sharing. Citation URLs and explanations are included in PowerPoint speaker notes. The deck has no video or animation.

The fourth slide contains a native editable scatter chart. It plots the exact payoff change 0.4p − 1.98(0.08p + 0.40p²), zoomed to adoption probabilities 0–0.5. Its highlighted points are the restricted optimum and symmetric equilibrium. Marker-series coordinates are repeated to match series lengths for compatibility with presentation viewers; they represent single exact points, not repeated observations.

The fifth slide describes Proposition 6.1 and its illustrative r=1 calibration. It assumes opponents follow the specified equilibrium strategy and each agent observes only their own two signals.
